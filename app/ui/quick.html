Your cover

Let Gemini design your cover: 4 easy steps

Gemini designs the cover for your book. GazBook then makes the same cover in every language, with the same picture, fonts and colours. (You can skip this and keep the simple cover.)

  1. Copied! Now press "2. Open Gemini".
  2. In Gemini: press Ctrl + V to paste, then Enter. Gemini gives you the cover, the same picture without words, and a design card. Save the picture without words (Download). If Gemini gives only one picture, type: "Now give me the same picture with no words at all."

    The Gemini app is not on this computer.

    After installing, press "2. Open Gemini" again. Sign in with your Gmail.

  3. 3. Paste the design card from Gemini (the lines starting with TITLE:, SUBTITLE:, AUTHOR:, BACK:)
